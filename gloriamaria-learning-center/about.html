About Us

Learning should be free and fun for every child.

Our mission

GloriaMaria Youth Learning Center gives free lessons to kinder kids. We help children learn the alphabet, count from 1 to 10, and explore colors, shapes, animals, days, and months using colors and pictures they love.

What kids can learn

The alphabet

All 26 letters, from A for Apple to Z for Zebra.

Numbers

Counting from 1 to 10 with pictures.

Vowels

Kids can find the vowels A, E, I, O, and U.

Colors & Shapes

Red, blue, green and more, plus circles, squares, and stars.

Animals

Meet farm and wild animals and hear the sounds they make.

Days & Months

Learn the 7 days of the week and the 12 months of the year.
